# Guilds

Decided by Joe on October 3, 2026. Builds as its own step right after Module 4 (Factions). Guilds were dropped from the first plan because factions already give everyone a side; they're back because they do something factions can't: let streamers from **different factions** form a team.

A guild is a group of streamers: a stream team, a creator collective, or a real org (an esports team or community that streams together). Viewers follow a guild to see all its members' streams in one place.

Brand sponsorships and deals are a separate, later feature (Phase 4, with ads), not part of guilds.

## Who can do what

- **Create a guild:** a verified account with 2FA that has streamed on S.V.E.R at least once. A person can lead (own) one guild.
- **Members:** streamers, from any faction. A streamer can be in up to **3 guilds** and picks one to show as their chat badge.
- **Viewers:** follow a guild. Following a guild shows its live members in the viewer's Following list and sidebar.
- **Roles inside a guild:** Leader (one; can transfer leadership), Officers (chosen by the leader), Members.

## Joining is by application

- Every member joins through an **application**. A streamer opens the guild page, writes a short message (up to 500 characters) and applies. Leaders and officers review applications in the guild's settings and accept or decline, with an optional note.
- Leaders and officers can **invite a streamer to apply**; the streamer still sends the application, so nobody is added without choosing to join.
- A guild can close applications for a while ("Not recruiting").
- One open application per streamer per guild; a declined streamer can apply again after 7 days.
- Applications from a streamer the guild has blocked, or from an account that is banned or restricted, are refused.
- Members can leave at any time. Leaders and officers can remove members (officers can't remove other officers or the leader). Every membership change is logged for the guild.

## What a guild has at launch

- **Guild page** at `sver.tv/g/{name}`: banner, avatar, tagline, about, members (live first, with each member's faction crest), a combined schedule of members' streams, the Apply button, and Follow.
- **Guild emblem badge:** each guild uploads a custom emblem (its avatar). In chat, a member's chosen guild shows as that emblem, a small badge next to their name after their faction crest, instead of a text tag. Hovering or tapping it shows the guild's name and links to its page. Members can hide it.
  - The guild's 2 to 5 character tag (for example `NOW`) stays as the emblem's label: it's what screen readers announce, and it shows in place of the emblem where images can't (Linked chat replies, notifications, plain-text views).
  - Emblems are square PNG or WebP, at least 112 px, shown at 18 px in chat and larger on the guild page and member lists. They follow the same review, report and removal rules as custom emotes, and can't imitate S.V.E.R, faction crests or verified marks.
- **Team shortcuts:** in Creator Studio, quick raid, host and co-stream invite with guildmates who are live, and an option to add guildmates to the auto-host list.
- **Verified orgs:** staff can verify a guild that represents a real organization (esports team, creator collective, company), shown with a verified mark. Verification requests go through the admin queue.

## Names and safety

- Guild names (3 to 40 characters), URL names and tags are unique, follow the username and banned-word rules, and can't impersonate staff, factions or verified orgs.
- Guild pages and text can be reported like profiles; staff can rename, reset or disband a guild that breaks the Community Guidelines.
- If a leader's account is deleted or banned, leadership passes to the longest-serving officer, or the guild is archived if there is none.

## What guilds don't do

- No effect on MAGNet or faction influence. Guilds are about streamers backing each other, not about discovery advantages.
- No shared money, guild currency or guild-wide subscriptions at launch.
- No brand deals (Phase 4, "Sponsorships").

## Legacy notes

The legacy guilds module allowed one guild per person with invite-only recruitment. Kept: name, URL name, tag, tagline, branding, verified flag, member titles. Changed: up to 3 guilds, joining only by application, cross-faction teams as the purpose.

## Done when

A streamer creates a guild; others apply and are accepted or declined by the leader and officers; an invited streamer applies and joins; a streamer belongs to 3 guilds and shows one guild's emblem badge in chat, with the tag as its label; the guild page shows live members first and a combined schedule; viewers follow a guild and see its live members; team shortcuts raid, host and invite guildmates; staff verify an org and can rename or disband a guild; nothing about guilds changes MAGNet or influence.
