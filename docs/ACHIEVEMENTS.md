There are 120 achievements in 10 categories, at least 3 per category in each of 4 bands (Joe, October 22, 2026). Viewer categories (Community, Scouting, Orders and loyalty, Levels, Support given) are banded by account level: 1+, 25+, 50+ and 100+. Streamer categories (Streaming, Audience, Raids, Subscribers, Tiers and dedication) are banded by creator tier, Scout to Pathfinder, with numbers sized to that tier's requirements ([SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) "Creator tiers"). A band is a difficulty step, not a gate: anyone can earn any achievement. Followers count verified followers ([PROFILES.md](PROFILES.md)), the number tiers use; viewers count Trusted viewers. All numbers are **Proposed**; keys never change, so earned rows stay valid.

### Unlocking, progress and rarity (October 22, 2026)

- **Unlock notice.** Earning an achievement sends a site notification ("Achievement unlocked: First Ten") that opens your profile. When a brand-new achievement is added, the first pass grants it to everyone who already qualifies without notices, so a release never floods the bell.
- **Locked and progress.** On your own profile and in Studio → Growth you see every achievement, earned or not, with your progress toward each (for example 6 / 10). Visitors see only what you've earned, since progress can reveal private records such as subscriptions.
- **Rarity.** Each achievement shows the share of accounts that hold it ("12% of accounts"), counting accounts that aren't deleted or internal.

### Streamer growth (Studio → Growth)

One page for growing a channel:

- **Creator tier progress:** the tier, the next tier's requirements against your last 90 days, and the ladder (moved here from Payouts, which links to it).
- **This week vs last week:** streams, hours streamed, average and peak Trusted viewers, new verified followers and new subscribers, each with the change from the 7 days before; plus unique Trusted viewers over the last 7 days.
- **Streamer achievements:** grouped by category and band, with progress.
- **Tier promotion notice:** a promotion sends a site notification and an email ([NOTIFICATIONS.md](NOTIFICATIONS.md) "Creator tier promotion").

## Cosmetics (Settings → Cosmetics)

- **Name style in chat**, always in your faction's colors: Plain (everyone), Bold (level 10), Small caps (level 20), Accent underline (level 30), Outlined (level 50).
- **Badge frame:** any frame you've unlocked (a new one every 10 levels); the newest is used until you choose.
- **Showcase:** up to 3 of your achievements on your profile and your user card.

Cosmetics never affect MAGNet, rotation, influence or money.

## Done when

1. Each achievement is earned exactly once, by doing the thing, and never from a client report.
2. A style or frame above your level, or an achievement you don't have, can't be chosen.
3. The chosen style shows in chat, the frame on the player and user cards, the showcase on the profile and user card.
4. Earning sends one notice per achievement (none for a backfill of a new one); the owner sees locked achievements with progress, visitors don't; every achievement shows its rarity.
5. Studio → Growth shows tier progress, the week-over-week numbers and streamer achievement progress; a tier promotion notifies by site and email.
